About Sereinora

An independent practice that writes music-license audits for US restaurants, bars and hotels.

Who prepares your audit

Every audit is prepared and signed by Sereinora’s founder. You deal with the same person from your first email to the final report.

The founder’s name appears on your written confirmation and on your report. It is kept off this website.

Why the work exists

A US venue can hold several music licenses and still leave some uses unlicensed: each PRO licenses only its own members’ songs (SESAC), and a music service generally covers only the music it supplies (Pandora CloudCover). The rules sit across federal law, four performing rights organizations and each music service’s own terms. Sereinora puts them side by side for one venue, in plain English, with the source for every statement.

How we work

Every rule is cited

Each legal or numeric statement points to its source: the US Code, the U.S. Copyright Office, or the provider’s own published terms.

Independent

Sereinora is not affiliated with ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR or any music service, and earns no commission or referral fee from any of them.

Clear about our role

Sereinora is not a law firm. We do not negotiate with PROs, clear rights or promise savings. We say plainly when a question needs a US attorney.

Your documents stay private

Documents you send are used only for your audit. Your name and results are never published without your written permission.
