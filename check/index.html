01 / What you receive

One page, in writing, within five working days.

  1. Our read of the situation. Decision gap, supplier workflow, specialist question, or no change needed — with the reason.
  2. Three questions for your current supplier or team. So you can test whether what you already pay for resolves it.
  3. Whether a paid review would be worth it. And, as plainly, when it would not.

The first read is based only on what you send. It is not a diagnosis, quotation or ranking of suppliers.

02 / Send one situation

Start with one room and one recent moment.

Unknown answers are fine — write “unknown”. Please do not include guest names, recordings or confidential information.

Opening your email app does not send anything. The message goes to contact@sereinora.com only when you press send.

Email us directly: write to contact@sereinora.com with the number of sites, the room, up to three moments it serves, one dated situation and its consequence, what is in place today, and who decides.

03 / If more is needed

A bounded paid review — only if the first read supports it.

An Independent Atmosphere Decision Brief considers one environment, up to three operating moments, your current supplier and the option of no change. It gives written recommendations, a proposed exception owner and comparison criteria, with one review and correction round.

For groups, we can scope whether the same rules should carry across sites. Fee and timing are set after a scope review; nothing is agreed by sending a situation.
