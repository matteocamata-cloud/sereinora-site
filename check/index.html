01 / Why it matters

Music in a US venue is licensed several times over.

Playing recorded or live music in a business is a public performance. Songwriters' rights are licensed by performing-rights organizations (PROs) — mainly ASCAP, BMI, SESAC and GMR — and one PRO's license does not cover another's songs.

$4,500average yearly music licensing reported by restaurant-association members
~200%rise in PRO costs per hotel 2021–2025 reported by one global chain
$750–$150,000statutory damages per song infringed (the top of the range requires wilfulness)

Sources: Bloomberg via IBJ (Jul 2025); 17 U.S.C. §504. See sources.

03 / Limits

What this check is — and is not.

It applies published rules of thumb to your answers. It is general information, not legal advice, and it cannot see your contracts. Sereinora is not a law firm and does not negotiate with PROs. If you have received a lawsuit, a demand with a deadline, or a letter from a lawyer, speak to a US attorney first.
