02 / How it works

Four steps, all by email.

  1. Request. Email us, or send your answers from the free check.
  2. Intake. You get a short form: rooms, music sources, live nights, and copies of your music service contract and any PRO invoices or letters.
  3. Audit. We map every use against every license and your music service terms.
  4. Report. A written report with the overlaps, the gaps, the exposure range and what to do, in order. One round of questions by email is included.

03 / What the report contains

Specific to your venue, not a generic guide.

Music use map
Each room and each moment of the day: what plays, from where, how loud, and who changes it.
License table
ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR and your music service: held, needed, overlapping or missing.
Exposure range
What a gap could cost under US copyright law, stated as a range, never as a prediction.
Savings to verify
Where you may be paying twice, with the exact question to ask each provider before you cancel anything.
Action list
Ten actions in order, with who to contact and what to keep on file.
Volume and daypart rules
A one-page operating sheet so your team plays the right music at the right level at the right time.
