How it works

  1. Request

    Email us, or send your answers from the free check. We confirm scope and price in writing.

What to have ready

None of it needs to be perfect. Tell us what you do not know.

  • Your music service contract, or just its name and the plan you are on.
  • Any invoices, license agreements or letters from ASCAP, BMI, SESAC or GMR.
  • A list of rooms and areas where music plays, and who controls it.
  • Live music, DJ and karaoke nights: how often and in which room.
  • The venue’s approximate floor area, and how many TVs and speakers it has.
  • Whether you post videos with music on social media.

Questions

Is this legal advice?

No. Sereinora is not a law firm. The audit is a factual map of what you play, what you hold and where the two do not match. If you have a lawsuit, a demand with a deadline or a lawyer’s letter, speak to a licensed attorney first. Our report gives them the facts.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Everything runs by email: a short intake form, the documents you already have, a written report, and one round of follow-up questions.

What do you need from me?

Your music service contract (or its name), any PRO invoices or letters, a list of rooms and live-music nights, the venue’s approximate floor area, and whether you post videos with music.

Can you promise I will save money?

No. The report shows where you may be paying twice and gives you the exact question to ask each provider, in writing, before you cancel anything.

What happens to the documents I send?

They are used only for your audit. We never publish your name or results without your written permission. See the privacy page for details, including deletion on request.

What if the audit finds a gap?

The report states the exposure as a range under US copyright law and lists ten actions in order. It does not predict what a PRO or a court would do.

Which venues do you work with?

Restaurants, bars and hotels. Audits currently cover venues in the United States; more countries are coming. The audit covers background music, live music, DJs, karaoke, TVs and music in your social videos. The audit is built for hospitality venues.

How and when do I pay?

After you request an audit, we reply by email with a written confirmation of scope and price, and instructions for paying. Work starts once payment is confirmed. Prices are in US dollars and exclude any license fees you decide to pay.

What if the report is late?

If we miss the delivery date we confirmed in writing, you get a full refund. The delivery clock starts when we have your payment and the documents we asked for.
