What Sereinora does

We write a report about one venue, or a group of venues. It lists every way the venue uses music and compares that with the licenses it holds and its music service’s terms. You see what is covered, where you may pay twice, and what is missing.

What Sereinora does not do

  • Give legal advice. Sereinora is not a law firm.
  • Negotiate with ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR or any music service on your behalf.
  • Clear rights or certify that you are compliant.
  • Promise savings or outcomes.

Who it is for

Restaurants, bars and hotels. The audit covers background music, live music, DJs, karaoke, TVs and music in your social videos.

How we work with you

Everything runs by email: no call and no site visit. You get written answers you can keep and forward.

How to order

  1. Send a request. Use the request form: it writes the email for you, and you send it from your own email app. You can also write to contact@sereinora.com from any email account. Include the venue name, the city and state, and the number of venues.
  2. Get a written confirmation. We reply by email with the scope, the price and instructions for paying.
  3. Pay. Work starts once payment is confirmed.
  4. Send your documents. A short intake form, plus the documents listed under What to send us.
  5. Receive your report by email, on the date we confirmed.

The request form does not send or store anything itself. Nothing reaches us until you press send in your email app.

Paying

  • You pay after we confirm the scope and price in writing, never before.
  • The payment instructions come in that confirmation email.
  • Prices are in USD.
  • Work starts once payment is confirmed.
  • You receive an invoice for every payment.

Cancelling

  • Changed your mind after paying? If you have not sent your documents yet, ask by email and we refund you in full.
  • If we have not received your documents 30 days after your payment, we write to you. You can then cancel for a full refund.
  • Once you have sent your documents and work has started, the refund promise under Delivery times applies.

What to send us

None of it needs to be perfect. Tell us what you do not know.

  • Your music service contract, or just its name and the plan you are on.
  • Any invoices, license agreements or letters from ASCAP, BMI, SESAC or GMR.
  • A list of rooms and areas where music plays, and who controls it.
  • Live music, DJ and karaoke nights: how often and in which room.
  • The venue’s approximate floor area, and how many TVs and speakers it has.
  • Whether you post videos with music on social media.

Send only documents you are allowed to share. We use them only to prepare your audit and answer your follow-up questions.

Your report

A written report about your venue, not a general guide. It has six parts:

  1. Music use map. Each room and each moment of the day: what plays, from where, how loud, and who changes it.
  2. License table. ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, GMR and your music service: held, needed, overlapping or missing.
  3. Exposure range. What a gap could cost under copyright law, stated as a range from the statute, never as a prediction.
  4. Where you may pay twice. Each possible double payment, with the exact question to ask the provider in writing before you cancel anything.
  5. Action list. Ten actions in order, with who to contact and what to keep on file.
  6. Volume and daypart rules. A one-page operating sheet so your team plays the right music at the right level at the right time.

See every part filled in on the sample report. The sample venue is made up.

Format

A PDF sent by email, prepared and signed by Sereinora’s founder. The founder’s name appears on your report and your written confirmation.

What a report can and cannot tell you

A report reflects the documents and facts you give us and the published law cited in it, as of the day it is delivered. It lists facts and the questions to ask each provider. It does not decide whether a license, exemption or legal threshold applies to you. Those questions are for the provider concerned or a licensed attorney.

After the report

  • You decide what to do with it.
  • Before you cancel any license or service, ask the provider the question listed in your report, in writing.
  • Ask us one round of questions by email within 30 days of delivery: it is included.
  • Want a new audit later, for example after you add live music or new rooms? It is a new audit at the published price.
  • If you need legal advice, give the report to a licensed attorney. It gives them the facts.

Letters and deadlines

A PRO letter, invoice or email

Read what to do when a PRO letter arrives. The PRO Letter Pack ($295, 48 hours) explains what they are asking, what you actually play and the questions to put back to them.

A lawsuit, a lawyer’s letter or a deadline

Speak to a licensed attorney first, without waiting. If useful, the PRO Letter Pack can give your attorney a factual summary.
