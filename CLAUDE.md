# sereinora-site

Public static site for sereinora.com, served by GitHub Pages from `main` (root). `CNAME` = sereinora.com. No build step, no dependencies, no cookies, no analytics, no forms that send data. Two opt-in exceptions, both disclosed on /privacy/: the assistant's AI mode sends chat messages to the Cloudflare Worker in `assistant/` and to Anthropic, and the language button opens pages through Google Translate.

Internal files stay off the public site through `_config.yml`: Pages runs Jekyll only to drop `CLAUDE.md`, `tools/`, `assistant/` and anything starting with `.` or `_` (such as `.claude/`). Never add front matter (`---`) or `{{ }}`/`{% %}` to a page, because Jekyll would then rewrite it. Add any new internal root file to `exclude:`. The repo itself stays readable on GitHub, so keep secrets and personal details out of it.

## Market and offer (since 11 Nov 2026)
US only: restaurants, bars and hotels. Funnel: `/check/` (free Music Exposure Check, runs in the browser) → `/audit/` → `/request/` (request form that composes an email; nothing is sent by the site) (Venue Music Audit $495, Group Audit $1,950 for up to 10 venues, PRO Letter Pack $295) → email to contact@sereinora.com. Answer pages under `/answers/` bring search traffic.

## Rules for any change
- Sereinora is not a law firm. Never write legal advice, never promise savings, rights clearance or outcomes, never claim to negotiate with PROs. Every page keeps the "not legal advice" footer.
- Every legal or numeric claim cites a source on the page. Check statute text on law.cornell.edu before changing §110(5)(B) or §504 figures.
- No client names, results or testimonials without written permission.
- Prices change only with the founder's approval.
- Keep pages dependency-free and accessible: test with Playwright at 375 px and 1280 px, no console errors, no broken internal links, no horizontal scroll.
- Publishing (push to `main`) needs the founder's approval for new public copy.

## Structure (since 12 Nov 2026 revamp)
- `/` sells the audit. The old art-led homepage is preserved intact at `/elements/` (uses `styles.css` + `site.js`).
- All commercial and legal pages share `assets/venue.css` + `assets/venue.js` (self-hosted fonts in `assets/fonts/`, no third-party requests). Typefaces: Source Serif 4 (optical sizes) for headings and figures, Mona Sans for text and UI, Bodoni Moda only for the "Sereinora" wordmark. Avoid Inter/Geist-style defaults. Header and footer live in `tools/partials/`; run `node tools/sync-chrome.mjs` after changing them and `node tools/qa.mjs` before every publish (see `tools/README.md`).
- `/about/` is public and linked. The founder's name is kept off the website at the founder's request: it appears only on client confirmations and reports. Do not add a name, location or registration claim without the founder's say-so. Once INPI registration is confirmed (SIRET issued), add the business details the founder approves (French "mentions légales" likely apply).
- Every legal or numeric claim cites a source on the page. Sources were verified on 12 Nov 2026 (statute text, NRA/AHLA figures via Bloomberg/IBJ, Brownstein alert, provider pricing and terms). Re-verify before changing any figure, and update the "Last reviewed" date.
- Terms and Privacy keep the Google/YouTube API text verbatim: it supports the Google OAuth verification. Edit only the venue-audit sections above it.
- The sample report at `/sample/` is fictional and must stay labeled as such. Never present it, or any figure in it, as a real client or result.
- Assistant: `assets/assistant.js` (button and panel on every page; `AI_ENDPOINT` empty = built-in answers only) and `assistant/` (Cloudflare Worker, rules in `assistant/worker/src/prompt.js`, eval in `assistant/worker/eval/`). Run `node assistant/build-knowledge.mjs` and redeploy the Worker after any page change so the assistant matches the site. Never loosen the legal-advice, pricing or refund rules in the prompt.
- Language button: machine translation through Google Translate (`translate.goog`) into the 17 languages most spoken at home in the US (U.S. Census Bureau, ACS-50, Table 1), English is the reference version. Extend the list in `assets/venue.js` when the market grows beyond the US.
- Assistant built-in mode searches `assets/assistant-index.json` (generated by `assistant/build-knowledge.mjs`); curated answers for key intents live at the top of `assets/assistant.js` and must use exact site wording.
- Free resources: `/checklist/` (printable) and `/answers/glossary/`; they may only reuse facts and sources already on the answer pages. After adding a page: add it to `sitemap.xml`, `assistant/build-knowledge.mjs` and run `node tools/og.mjs`.

## Social media
Social accounts are run by the `sereinora-social` agent (`.claude/agents/sereinora-social.md`). It drafts and recommends, and it publishes only posts the founder has approved one by one. Every rule above applies to posts, replies and bios.
