# sereinora-site

Public static site for sereinora.com, served by GitHub Pages from `main` (root). `CNAME` = sereinora.com. No build step, no dependencies, no cookies of its own (only Google's opt-in language cookie), no analytics, no forms that send data. Two opt-in exceptions, both disclosed on /privacy/: the assistant's AI mode sends chat messages to the Cloudflare Worker in `assistant/` and to Anthropic, and choosing a language loads Google's website translator on the page (it sets a `googtrans` cookie).

Internal files stay off the public site through `_config.yml`: Pages runs Jekyll only to drop `CLAUDE.md`, `tools/`, `assistant/` and anything starting with `.` or `_` (such as `.claude/`). Never add front matter (`---`) or `{{ }}`/`{% %}` to a page, because Jekyll would then rewrite it. Add any new internal root file to `exclude:`. The repo itself stays readable on GitHub, so keep secrets and personal details out of it.

Never delete or rename `google9b2b52b82e96b0c8.html` (Google Search Console ownership, verified 13 Nov 2026) or `95cfccb5e7fc64375bd765d8f7fb179c.txt` (IndexNow key): removing either breaks search-engine access. Run `node tools/indexnow.mjs` after each publish.

## Market and offer (since 11 Nov 2026)
Mission: worldwide. Sereinora is for restaurants, bars and hotels anywhere; never write it as a US-only business. Current coverage: audits, the check and the answer pages use US rules, and outreach is US-only for now. Say so honestly where someone could buy ("Audits currently cover venues in the United States; more countries are coming"), never apply US rules to a venue elsewhere, and invite other countries to write in. Legal facts stay precise about their jurisdiction ("US law allows…"); positioning stays global ("for restaurants, bars and hotels"); say "a licensed attorney", not "a US attorney". Since 13 Nov 2026 (founder: "remove all the US US US mentions"), each page with legal content states its jurisdiction once, with the label in the page header (`<p class="scope"><b>Rules: United States</b><a href="/coverage/">Other countries</a></p>`, style `.scope` in `assets/venue.css`). Do not repeat "US" in running text or positioning. Keep it inline only where a sentence states a statute figure or threshold (§504 damages, §110(5) limits), and leave citations, proper names and quotes as they are. Prices say "USD". Funnel: `/check/` (free Music Exposure Check, runs in the browser) → `/audit/` → `/request/` (request form that composes an email; nothing is sent by the site) (Venue Music Audit $495, Group Audit $1,950 for up to 10 venues, PRO Letter Pack $295) → email to contact@sereinora.com. Answer pages under `/answers/` bring search traffic.

## Countries (since 13 Nov 2026)
- Coverage today: United States only. `/coverage/` ("Where we work") is the single page that states it; update it first when coverage changes.
- The check (`check/check.js`) and the request form (`assets/request.js`) ask where the venue is first. "Another country" never gets a US result or a price: the check stops and offers a "Coverage request" email; the form sends a "Coverage request: <country>" email with no estimate. Count these emails: they decide the next country. Do not pick a country without that data and the founder's say-so.
- URL plan for a new country (do not build before the founder approves the country): the current root pages stay the US version. Each new country gets its own folder named by its ISO 3166-1 code in lower case (for example `/gb/`, `/ca/`, `/au/`), with its own check rules, audit scope, prices in local currency, sources from that country's law and organizations, and "Last reviewed" dates. Never copy US legal text into a country folder. Shared pages (`/about/`, `/coverage/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/`) stay at the root and list every country.
- When a second country exists: add `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-us">` / `hreflang="en-gb"` (etc.) plus `x-default` pointing at `/coverage/` on every page that has a counterpart, list both URLs in `sitemap.xml`, add the country to the check's and the form's country question, to `assistant/build-knowledge.mjs` and to the assistant's coverage rule in `assistant/worker/src/prompt.js`. A different language is the translator's job (`?lang=xx`), not a separate country folder.

## Rules for any change
- Sereinora is not a law firm. Never write legal advice, never promise savings, rights clearance or outcomes, never claim to negotiate with PROs. Every page keeps the "not legal advice" footer.
- Every legal or numeric claim cites a source on the page. Check statute text on law.cornell.edu before changing §110(5)(B) or §504 figures.
- No client names, results or testimonials without written permission.
- Prices change only with the founder's approval.
- Keep pages dependency-free and accessible: test with Playwright at 375 px and 1280 px, no console errors, no broken internal links, no horizontal scroll.
- Publishing (push to `main`) needs the founder's approval for new public copy.
