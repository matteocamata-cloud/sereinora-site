Where we work

Sereinora is for restaurants, bars and hotels anywhere. Audits currently cover venues in the United States; more countries are coming.

Covered today: the United States

If your venue is in the United States, everything on this site applies to you:

  • the free Music Exposure Check,
  • the Venue Music Audit, the Group Audit and the PRO Letter Pack,
  • the answers and the licensing checklist.

All of them are written against US law and the four US performing rights organizations: ASCAP, BMI, SESAC and GMR.

Venue in another country

We do not audit it yet, and we will not apply US rules to it. Our audits are built on US law and the US organizations, so a US answer could be wrong for your venue.

Tell us where your venue is. We add countries where venues ask for us, so every request counts, and we will write to you when we cover yours.

Questions

Can I use the free check for a venue outside the US?

No. The check asks where your venue is first. It uses US rules, so it stops for any other country and offers to put you on the list instead.

Can I buy an audit now and use it abroad?

No. An audit maps a venue against US licensing. For a venue elsewhere it would not tell you what you need, so we do not sell one.

I run venues in the US and in other countries.

We can audit the US venues now. Say which other countries you operate in, and we will tell you when we cover them.

Which country comes next?

It is not decided. We will choose by where requests come from, and we will update this page when it is.
