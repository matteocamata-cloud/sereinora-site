Answers / Exemption

Is my venue exempt from music licensing?

Possibly — but only for radio and TV broadcasts, and only within strict limits.

On this page

The ruleNever coveredNext step

01

The rule (17 U.S.C. §110(5)(B)).

Food and drink venues
Under 3,750 gross sq ft (excluding parking): broadcasts exempt with no equipment limits.
Other businesses
Under 2,000 gross sq ft: broadcasts exempt with no equipment limits.
Larger venues
Still exempt only if audio uses no more than 6 speakers in total (no more than 4 in one room or adjoining space), and TVs number no more than 4 (one per room, 55-inch maximum).
Always
No direct charge to hear the music; no retransmission beyond the venue; the broadcast itself must be licensed.

02

What it never covers.

  • Streaming services, downloads, CDs or vinyl.
  • Live music, DJs or karaoke.
  • Music you add to social media or marketing.

Summary of the statute for general information; check the current text before relying on it.
