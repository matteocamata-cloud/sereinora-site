Can I play Spotify in my restaurant or bar?

Not with a personal account. Here is why, and what the legal options look like.

On this page

Why notOptionsNext step

01

Why a personal account is not enough.

Personal streaming plans (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Pandora) are licensed for personal, non-commercial listening. Playing them to customers is a public performance that the subscription does not cover.

02

The legal options.

Business music service
Typically around $17–$60 per location per month. Many include PRO licensing for the recorded background music they supply. Check the contract: live music, DJs and karaoke are not included.
Direct PRO licenses
Needed for live music, DJs and karaoke, and for recorded music not supplied by a licensed service. Each PRO covers only its own catalogue.
Radio or TV, small venue
Broadcasts can be exempt in small venues within equipment limits. See the rules.
