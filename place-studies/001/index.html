Why this decision

A clear welcome matters more than sonic novelty. New composition is only worth considering after the place has an identified need.

Evidence to collect

Existing levels and playlist; reception interruptions; speaker positions; staff observations at comparable arrival times.

Current substitute

A continuous lobby playlist from the existing provider, in one playback zone.

Operating boundaries

Venue-specific playback permission and staff controls must be verified. This study supplies no tracks, levels or equipment specification.

Attention before composition.

Start with the existing setup. Commission sound only for an identified need.

Study assumptions and limits +

The Paris lounge, layout and hours are fictional. Reception, shared seating, street-facing windows and one playback zone are assumed. Speaker and ventilation locations, controls and playback permissions are unverified.

No site visit, acoustic measurement, client result, licensed audio or improved guest outcome is claimed. Compare each option with the current setup in an authorized real venue. This study establishes neither paid demand nor the ability to deploy music.
